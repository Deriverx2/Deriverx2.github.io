The Second LoViF 2026 Challenge on Real-World All-in-One Image Restoration: Methods and Results↗

Xiang Chen, Hao Li, Jiangxin Dong, Jinshan Pan, …, E B Benson, et al.

arXiv:2607.21118 PDF

Presents a comprehensive review and benchmark of the second LoViF Challenge on Real-World All-in-One Image Restoration across diverse degradations including blur, low-light, haze, rain, and snow.
